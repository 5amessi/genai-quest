# Enterprise Vendor Approval Policy

Effective 2026-01-15. This version supersedes all earlier versions.

## Approval workflow

1. The request owner submits the Vendor Intake Form with business purpose, data types, owner, and estimated annual value.
2. Procurement performs due diligence and sanctions screening.
3. Security completes a risk assessment when the vendor processes company or customer data.
4. Legal reviews the contract and data-processing agreement.
5. Finance confirms budget availability.
6. The Procurement Director gives final approval before a purchase order is issued or access is granted.

## Evidence retention

The request owner retains the approved intake, risk assessment, contract, and purchase order in the vendor record for seven years.
